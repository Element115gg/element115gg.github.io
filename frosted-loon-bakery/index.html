Our story

Baked in the Land of 10,000 Lakes

Frosted Loon Bakery is a small, local Minnesota bakery. We named it after the state bird because, like the loon, we're happiest close to home.

Every cake, cookie, and cake pop is made by hand, one order at a time, with good ingredients and plenty of frosting. Big celebrations and small "just because" moments get the same care.

  • Made to OrderBaked fresh for your date, never sitting on a shelf.
  • Small BatchHands-on care and attention to the details.
  • Minnesota MadeLocal, homegrown, and proud of it.
